ℹ️ How to Play

1. Tap to Move: Tap any word in the pool to send it to the next empty slot.

2. Select & Swap: Tap a placed word (orange) and then tap another word or empty slot to swap.

3. Long-Press & Drag: Hold down on a placed word (purple) and drag to insert and shift words.

4. Auto-Validation: Sentence checks automatically when filled!
